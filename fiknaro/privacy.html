Fiknaro privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Fiknaro is a free, offline learning app for Android with interactive lessons in maths, science and programming, package io.github.husseinadq.fiknaro. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What we collect

Nothing. Fiknaro has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no servers that receive information about you. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

Your progress and settings

The lessons you finish, the days you practised, and your settings (language, theme, sound) are saved only in the app's own storage on your phone. If you have turned on Android's backup in your phone's settings, Android may include this data in your own device backup, which is handled by Google under your Google account, not by us. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes it.

Internet use

All courses are included in the app, and this version of Fiknaro makes no network connections. Fiknaro asks for Internet access so that a later version can download more courses from our course server; such downloads only fetch course files and send no information about you or your progress. As with any download, the server would receive your device's IP address. This policy will be updated before a version that downloads courses is released.

Sharing

If you use "Share app", Fiknaro hands a copy of the app to Android's share sheet, and you choose where it goes. If you import a course file, Fiknaro reads only the file you pick.

Permissions

Fiknaro asks for one permission, Internet access, for the course downloads described above. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files you did not choose, or the advertising ID.

Children

Fiknaro is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If Fiknaro's handling of data changes, for example if an optional account for syncing progress is added, this page and the app's Google Play listing will be updated before that version is released.
