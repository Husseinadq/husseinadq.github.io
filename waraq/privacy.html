Waraq privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Waraq (ورق) is a free, offline document app for Android, package io.github.husseinadq.waraq. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What we collect

Nothing. Waraq has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no servers of its own. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

Your documents

Files you open are read on your device. To keep your original safe, Waraq keeps a private working copy, your marks and drafts in the app's own storage on your phone. They are excluded from device backups and never leave your phone unless you choose to save or share a file through Android's save screen or share sheet. Removing a document from Waraq, or uninstalling the app, deletes this data.

Internet use

Waraq uses the internet for one thing only: when you ask it to download an optional dictionary, it fetches that file over HTTPS from GitHub (github.com/Husseinadq/waraq-dictionaries) and checks it against a fixed checksum. No information about you or your documents is sent. As with any download, GitHub receives your device's IP address and handles it under the GitHub Privacy Statement. If you never download a dictionary, Waraq makes no network connections.

Permissions

Waraq asks for one permission, Internet access, used only for the dictionary download above. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or advertising ID.

Children

Waraq is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If Waraq's handling of data ever changes, for example if a future version shows ads, this page and the app's Google Play listing will be updated before that version is released.
